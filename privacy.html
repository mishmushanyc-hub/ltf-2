Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Life Thru Food ("LTF," "we," "us") hosts Sittings and Sessions: dinners built around a Subject and the people who gather to share a meal. This policy explains what personal information we collect through lifethrufood.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions can go to info@lifethrufood.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what you give us through our forms and checkout, plus basic technical data.

  • Identifiers and contact details: name, email address, mobile phone number, and Instagram handle.
  • Event details: seats reserved, party size, +1 name and phone number, invite codes, dietary notes, and how you heard about us.
  • Inquiry content: information you submit through our private event, sponsor, artist submission, and artist nomination forms.
  • Payment information: for paid Sittings, payment is handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location from your IP address.

How we use it

  • To confirm seats, invite requests, and inquiries, and to send the address and details for events you are attending.
  • To send logistics and updates about events you have signed up for, by email and, if you opt in, by text message.
  • To send our newsletter and announcements if you joined the list. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To run, secure, and improve the site, and to meet legal obligations.

Text messaging

If you provide your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we use it to send messages about events you have signed up for. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf and only as needed to run LTF:

  • Brevo: sends our transactional and newsletter emails.
  • Stripe: processes payments.
  • Supabase: stores our event, guest, and form records.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of LTF, our guests, or others.

Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to run events, keep our guest records, and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Ask to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing info@lifethrufood.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law, and we will honor them.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, though no system is completely secure. Our events and site are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
